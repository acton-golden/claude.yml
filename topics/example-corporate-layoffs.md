# Topic: TechCo Mass Layoffs — Q1 2025

## Subject
CEO Dana Mercer, TechCo Inc. — overseeing the company's largest-ever reduction in force: 4,200 employees (22% of workforce), announced January 15, 2025.

## Key Facts
- Announcement came 11 days after the company's holiday party, for which executives flew business class to Cancun.
- Q4 2024 earnings beat analyst expectations by 14%. The company cited "right-sizing for long-term growth."
- The CEO received a $6.2M retention bonus in December 2024, disclosed in a filing three weeks after layoffs.
- 60% of those laid off were in customer support and operations. Zero executive team members were cut.
- The company reported $1.4B in cash reserves at time of announcement.
- Several laid-off employees had been with the company 10+ years and were within 18 months of full pension vesting.
- Internal Slack messages (leaked to press) show VP of HR writing: "We need to move before the market softens further."

## Tension Points
- The gap between stated reason ("efficiency") and actual timing (pre-vest cutoffs)
- Whether the Cancun trip and retention bonuses reflect a two-tier company culture
- What "long-term growth" means when cuts hit customer-facing roles hardest
- The human cost: families, health insurance, mortgage defaults in the affected region

## Questions the Subject Will Dodge
- Why cut before vesting cliffs?
- Did executives take any pay cuts?
- Who specifically made the call and when?
- What was the board's role?
