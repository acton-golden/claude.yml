# Topic: Riverside City Water Contamination Cover-Up

## Subject
Commissioner Pat Ellison, Riverside City Water Authority — responsible for public water infrastructure since 2019.

## Key Facts
- Lead levels in Riverside's water tested at 22 ppb in June 2024 — the EPA action level is 15 ppb.
- The Water Authority received test results July 3. The public was not notified until September 18 — 77 days later.
- During that window, the city distributed bottled water to municipal offices but not to residential areas.
- Commissioner Ellison's department approved infrastructure reports calling the pipes "serviceable" in 2022 and 2023.
- A city engineer, Marcus Webb, filed an internal memo in March 2024 warning of corrosion risk. He was transferred to a different department in April.
- At least 340 children in affected zip codes were screened for lead exposure following the public announcement. Results are not yet public.
- The Water Authority received $8.3M in federal infrastructure funds in 2021 earmarked for pipe replacement. Auditors say $3.1M is unaccounted for.

## Tension Points
- The 77-day silence while officials had access to clean water
- What happened to Marcus Webb and why
- Where the $3.1M went
- The decision to notify offices before residents — who made it and why

## Questions the Subject Will Dodge
- Who decided to delay public notification?
- Did you know about Webb's memo before his transfer?
- Where did the $3.1M go?
- Have you personally apologized to any of the 340 children?
